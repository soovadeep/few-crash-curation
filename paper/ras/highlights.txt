- When most logs in a regime are wrong, no log-only rule can reliably identify them
- A failure run is used as a reference for the deployed dynamics, not as training data
- FACT removes every log whose gradient opposes the failure's, with no count to tune
- One crash dive cuts a quadrotor's crash rate from 99% to 3.1%; adding it leaves 95%
- Each assumption is mapped to where it holds, where it fails and what FACT does there
